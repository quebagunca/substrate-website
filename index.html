Substrate Sentinel

Shipment tracking and document filing for importers.

What we do

Substrate Sentinel is a service for import businesses. Customers send bills of lading, invoices, packing lists, and related shipping documents. We file each document against the shipment it belongs to, and we show that shipment’s status from the carrier, from vessel positions, and from checks a person has logged. No single source is treated as the only truth.

The service is for businesses we work with. It is not a public consumer product, and it is not used to send marketing messages.

WhatsApp

Customers and their staff message Substrate Sentinel on WhatsApp to send a document or to ask for a status update. We reply on that same conversation: where the document was filed, or the status they asked for. A status update is sent only when someone requests it.

Messaging the Substrate Sentinel WhatsApp number is how a customer opts in to those replies. Anyone can stop further messages by replying STOP, or by emailing us. See the privacy policy for what we receive from WhatsApp and who processes it.
